SCORING FACTORS

What should the score value?

Every score component is a coverage rate: the share of a selected population group that can reach at least one proposed hub. Expand a group to define access and importance.

The score is a weighted average of selected factor and travel mode coverage values. Each value is population coverage from 0 to 1. A block is counted once if any selected site serves it.
